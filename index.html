    What's under your feet

    Shropshire and the Marches pack an unusual amount of deep time into a small area — from 680-million-year-old volcanic rock on the Wrekin to Ice Age boulder clay in the valleys. The regions below are written up by outcrop; the timescale further down lets you place any of them, or any period at all, in context.
